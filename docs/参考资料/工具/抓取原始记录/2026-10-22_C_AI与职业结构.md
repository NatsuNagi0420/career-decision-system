  **① 职业级任务暴露指数的构造方法（§1.1–1.2、§2、§3）**
  - 底架是 ISCO-08 官方任务表（§1.1）：
    > To build the principal data frame of tasks and occupations, we use as our foundation Part III of the official ISCO-08 documentation, which provides detailed definition and description of tasks for each of the **436 ISCO-08 4-digit occupations** (ILO 2023b). … The number of ISCO-08 tasks assigned to each occupation can vary anywhere from **4 to 14**.
    表 1 给出的合计：Total ISCO tasks **3,123**、Total GPT tasks **4,360**、4-digit codes **436**。
  - 用 GPT-4 补生成任务（§1.2）：
    > We therefore adjust the prompt and request GPT-4 to generate a set of **10 typical tasks** for each of the 436 ISCO-08 4-digit occupations …
    > Some of the GPT-generated tasks are more generic than those given in ISCO … We suspect that the model, by being prompted to provide a list of ten tasks, might be deriving some additional tasks in order to complete the list.
  - **打分的题面与量纲（§1.2 末，这是"暴露度"的真正定义）**：
    > We request GPT-4 to generate **a score between 0 and 1, representing potential automation with GPT-based technology for each task** in the ISCO task collection and in the GPT-generated set of tasks. We provide the occupation's ISCO 4-digit code, **specify whether the job is located in a high-income or a low-income country** and ask the model to justify its decision.
    prompt 原句（逐字）：`"Provide a score of potential automation of this task with GPT technology, given that the job is located in a high[low] income country: " + "The score should range 0-1. … Do not give any ranges just one score for each task."`
  - **分档阈值（§3 首段）**：
    > Given the range of the estimated index (0-1), we consider scores **below 0.25 as representing very low exposure** and those between **0.25 and 0.5 as low exposure**. **Medium exposure** is captured in scores with the range of **0.5-0.75**, while tasks with scores **above 0.75 are considered as highly exposed**. The same cut-off points are applied to the occupation-level scores, calculated as **a mean score of the tasks** that belong to each occupation.
  - **职业级"自动化 vs 增强"分组公式（§3，式 1.1／1.2 —— 本条最可移植的东西）**：
    > Let µi and σi denote the mean and standard deviation of the task-level scores for a given occupation i … We define an occupation to have **"Augmentation potential"** if the following conditions are satisfied: **0.4 > µi and µi + σi > 0.5** (1.1)
    > Similarly, an occupation is said to have **"automation potential"** if it fulfils these criteria: **µi > 0.6 and µi - σi > 0.5** (1.2)
    表 5 的四象限：Low Mean+High SD＝Augmentation potential；High Mean+Low SD＝Automation potential；High Mean+High SD＝**"The big unknown"**；Low Mean+Low SD＝Not affected。
  - **一致性／稳健性检验（§2，表 4）**：
    > We conduct an additional test of scoring consistency … by making **100 predictions for 5 tasks** randomly selected from all tasks on ISCO-08 list. … The scores are highly consistent across different types of tasks, **with SDs not exceeding 0.05**.
    表 4 五行的分值（可当"什么算高分"的锚）：理发 `Cutting, washing, tinting and waving hair 0.06 ± 0.03`；金属清洗设备 `0.11 ± 0.04`；病历记录 `0.64 ± 0.05`；核单 `Verifying accuracy of documents and records relating to payments … 0.73 ± 0.05`；图书馆借还登记 `0.73 ± 0.05`。
    另一条平行检验（§2）：
    > we use a slightly modified prompt to generate occupational-level scores for **over 7,500 job titles** … These jobs do not have detailed tasks, but a comparison … reveals **a proximity across the board**.
  - **加权方式（§2 末，直接决定"分数能不能读成工时占比"）**：
    > a classic challenge … concerns attributing the **share of time** needed to execute the individual tasks … For our case, we opt for the most straightforward solution especially given the global focus, which is to **apply equal weights to each task-level sub-component or each occupation**.
  - 就业侧合成（§4.1）：
    > We use microdata for **59 countries** that report 4-digit microdata in ISCO-08 format: **8** low-income (LIC), **24** lower-middle-income (LMIC), **19** upper-middle-income (UMIC) and **8** high-income countries. We take **the latest year available for each country** …
    脚注 8：> We rely on **weighted means** … we also test calculations based on weighted-median. Since the results are stable and very similar in both cases, we keep the weighted mean.

  **② "多大比例职业受生成式 AI 影响"的数字（Abstract／§3／§4）**
  - 任务层（Abstract，逐字同 §3 正文）：
    > we find that **only the broad occupation of clerical work is highly exposed** to the technology with **24 per cent of clerical tasks considered highly exposed** and an additional **58 percent with medium-level exposure**. For the other occupational groups, the greatest share of highly exposed tasks oscillates **between 1 and 4 per cent**, and medium exposed tasks **do not exceed 25 per cent**. As a result, the most important impact of the technology is likely to be of **augmenting work** – automating some tasks within an occupation while leaving time for other duties – **as opposed to fully automating occupations**.
    §3 补一句口径：
    > If we also account for tasks with medium-level exposure (58 per cent of all tasks), a **full 82 per cent of clerical job tasks are exposed at an above-average level**. … **Even assuming large margins of error**, the result is still striking.
  - 就业层（自动化，按收入组）：
    > In low-income countries, only **0.4 per cent** of total employment is potentially exposed to automation effects, whereas in high-income countries the share rises to **5.5 percent**.（Abstract）
    §4.1 同段补性别：> the share of female participation in these occupations also grows with countries' income levels, and in the HICs it is **more than double the male share** of total employment.
  - 就业层（增强，按收入组）：
    > the greater impact is from augmentation, which has the potential to affect **10.4 percent** of employment in low-income countries and **13.4 percent** of employment in high-income countries.（Abstract）
    §4.1：> In the **LMICs**, such jobs have the highest share of the employment distribution, with **14.4 per cent** of total employment classified in this category.
    §4.2（全球估计，图 9a）：> **UMICs and HICs** having the largest share of employment with high augmentation potential (**13.5 and 13.4 per cent** respectively) and the **LICs** having the lowest share (**10.4 per cent**).
  - **"大未知"组（不能归到自动化也不能归到增强的那一档）**：
    > these occupations constitute a nontrivial share of the global employment, with some **16.2 per cent and 553 million workers** falling into this category.（§5，图 11b）
    > we find it important to quantify the share of employment belonging to this group. … While we **refrain from speculating on the direction** of this evolution …
  - 结论层的两句"净方向"表述（§6.1）：
    > Taking GPT's current capabilities at face value … gives us an **indicative picture** that suggests **greater potential for job augmentation as opposed to automation**. This finding represents a **continuum** with previous waves of technological progress, despite recurring bouts of anxiety.
    > In other words, **wealthier countries are likely to face both more disruptive effects** in the technological transition **and higher net gains** from the process.

  **③ 性别／教育／收入组梯度**
  - 性别（§4.2，图 9b，全球口径）：
    > we observe that **3.7 per cent** of all **female** employment in the world is in jobs that are potentially automatable with generative AI technology, compared with only **1.4 per cent of male** employment. In high-income countries, the share of potentially affected female jobs is **7.8 per cent, more than double the 2.9 per cent** of male jobs for that income group. At the same time, the share of jobs with **high augmentation potential is also greater among female than male jobs across all income groups**. This suggests that any form of technological transition would have a strongly gendered effect, with **a badly managed process disproportionately harming women**, and a well-managed transition potentially creating important opportunities in terms of women's empowerment.
    §4.1（图 7b，微观数据口径，与上句略有出入）：> in high-income countries, jobs with high automation potential constitute **8.5 per cent of female employment, compared to 3.9 per cent of male employment**.
    §6 复述时又写成 **7.9 per cent vs 2.9 per cent**（HIC）与 **2.7 per cent v 1.3 per cent**（UMIC）——**同一份文件里 HIC 女性自动化占比出现 7.8／7.9／8.5 三个值**，抄数时必须连小节号一起抄，不能只写"ILO 称 8%"。
  - 收入组（结构性解释，§5）：
    > the general trend is for the **share of clerical occupations to grow with income**, which explains the disproportionately higher potential automation effects in wealthier economies. For example, jobs of secretaries, accounting and bookkeeping clerks, or bank tellers and cashiers enjoy a **nearly linear relationship** between the country's income and the share of employment they take.
    > occupations of **contact centre salespersons and data entry clerks** are relatively more present in the **middle-income** countries than in the high-income countries, while the jobs of **application programmers are strongly dominated by men**.
    > In the Philippines, a half million people were employed in call centres in 2016, **of whom 53 percent were women** (DOLE, 2018).
  - 教育／技能层：本文件的梯度是**按 ISCO 大类**给的（不是按学历），§3：
    > highest exposure found amongst **clerical support workers**, followed by **technicians and associate professionals**, and by **professionals**. While these occupations have no official common category, they are broadly associated with **"knowledge work"** (Berg and Gmyrek 2023).
    > The results for **service and sales workers are more mixed**, with some occupations surpassing the threshold but most others falling below. **Plant and machine operators and assemblers, elementary occupations, craft and related trades workers and skilled agricultural, forestry and fishery workers have more limited exposure.**
    （**按受教育年限分组的梯度不在这份文件里**，见 C2/C3 的 ILO WESO 精炼指数与性别简报。）

  **④ 它自己声明的局限（§2 与 Abstract，逐字，本路最关键的一段）**
    > we believe that our exercise is likely to be estimating the **upper bound** of the exposure to GPT.
    > **tech experts tend to overstate technological capacities and risks** … GPT is likely to reflect **techno-optimism** and overstate some task-level scores. GPT-generated scores also **do not account for job-level task variation**, which can lower occupational-level scores (Arntz et al., 2017).
    > **our prompts to GPT focus on technical feasibility and ignore important determinants of technological diffusion**, including the feasibility of adoption in a given environment which is dependent on constraints such as access to electricity and internet … or local market dynamics, such as **relative cost of labour to technology**, level of digital literacy or access to finance.
    > despite having generated score predictions from prompts specifying whether the job is in a low- or a high-income country, we find that **the difference between the two is too small to justify the use of both datasets**. Therefore, for purposes of this initial analysis, **we use the high-income country scores**, with the understanding that this further contributes to estimating the upper bound of global exposure.
    > **The objective of this exercise is not to derive headline figures**, but rather to analyse the direction of possible changes …（§Introduction）
    > **We stress the focus of our work on the concepts of "exposure" and "potential", which does not imply automation**（同上，原文为 "does not imply automation, but rather lists occupations … for jobs that are more likely to be affected"）
    > **We stress that the primary value of this analysis is not the precise estimates**, but rather the insights that the overall distribution of such scores provides about the nature of possible changes.（Abstract）
    > The analysis **does not take into account the new jobs that will be created** … Twenty years ago, there were no social media managers … As demonstrated by Autor et al. (2022), **some 60 per cent of employment in 2018 in the United States was in jobs that did not exist in the 1940s**.
    > However, such effects **do not consider infrastructure constraints**, which will impede the possibility for use in lower-income countries and likely increase the productivity gap.（Abstract）
    图 9a 的读数纪律：> Given the data limitations, the exact numbers presented in Figure 9a should be read as **an indication of a general trend**.
    一处外部代价数（§6.1，转引）：> a study of the effects of automation on Dutch workers during 2010-2016, found that workers made redundant as a result of automation experienced a **5-year cumulative wage income loss of 9 per cent of an annual wage** (Bessen et al., 2019). The losses were **only partially offset** by various benefits systems.

- **局限与反面证据（本路补充，不只是它自陈的）**：
  1. **指数的"生产者"是被打分的模型本身**：任务清单与被打分对象有一部分是同一个 GPT-4 生成的（4,360 条 GPT 任务），ISCO 任务也只有 4–14 条；作者自己承认 GPT 任务"更泛"（more generic）且倾向给出更高分（> scores based on tasks previously generated by GPT tend to be higher than those attributed to tasks coming directly from ISCO-08）。它选 ISCO 任务版作基准，但这仍是**同一模型的两种自我陈述**，不是外部校准。
  2. **等权＋无时间权重** ⇒ 分数**不能读成"该职业 x% 的工时会被替代"**，只能读成"任务条目的平均技术可行性"。这条限制决定了 UI 上任何"暴露度百分比"都必须挂"不分工时"的脚注。
  3. **低收入国 prompt 生成了但没用**（"difference … too small"）——所有国家的分数其实都是**高收入国那一套**，收入组差异全部来自**职业结构差异**，不来自技术可行性差异；所以它给不出"中国 vs 美国同一职业暴露度不同"这类结论，产品里也不能暗示。
  4. **同一指标在文内有三个版本值**（女性 HIC 自动化占比 7.8／7.9／8.5），说明图的读数与文字表存在不一致；引用要指小节，不能只指"ILO"。
  5. **无中国专属结果**：只有收入组（LMIC/UMIC）与 59 国微观库的加权均值，**没有单独给出中国**（本轮未在 txt 中 grep 到 China 的分国数字），因此对中国用户只能当"同类收入组"的背景，不能当"中国这个职业如何"的依据。
  6. 反面证据（它自己给的）：**"暴露 ≠ 自动化"是原话**（"exposure … does not imply automation"）+ "upper bound" + 60% 的 2018 年美国就业在 1940 年代不存在的职业 ⇒ 这份文件本身就是"不能把暴露度当职业前景判决"的第一手出处。
- **能指到哪条产品决定**：
  - **新增一条依据链（索引 §一 的"链 6｜趋势／暴露度能不能上屏"）的核心件**：暴露度指数的构造是**"GPT-4 给 ISCO-08 任务打 0–1 分 + 等权均值 + mean±SD 四象限"**，它的产物是**方向分类**（augmentation / automation / big unknown / not affected），**不是岗位层证据**，也不是"前景好坏"。按 §A.2 归 **T3（聚合统计，只能进背景卡，TTL 12 个月）**，且要额外打一个 flag `is_model_scored_index`（区别于问卷/平台统计）。
  - 落到字段与判据：① `evidence_items` 里暴露度类记录**必须存 `index_construction`（如 `ILO-WP96-task-mean±SD`）与 `thresholds`（0.25/0.5/0.75）与 `weights='equal'`**，缺任一不许上屏；② 分类判据（式 1.1/1.2）是**职业级**，产品的"方向"若对应 ISCO 4-digit 才能挂这个数，对应自造方向名一律不挂；③ **UI 禁止把暴露度做成"红/绿"或"前景分"**——文件自己的措辞是 "does not imply automation"，产品若做成判决就是换了口径。
  - 可直接进背景卡的句子级依据：**"任务层 24%/58%（仅文职）"、"自动化就业占比 LIC 0.4% → HIC 5.5%"、"增强 10.4%–13.4%"、"big unknown 16.2%/5.53 亿"、"女性自动化敞口为全球 3.7% vs 男性 1.4%"**——每条都得带 `caliber`（任务层还是就业层、微观 59 国还是全球加权）与"上界估计"字样。

  **① 方法与口径（它用的是 C2 的指数，不是 C1 的）**
    > This brief uses the **ILO-NASK global index of occupational exposure to Gen AI (Gmyrek et al., 2025)** to analyse the potential impacts of Gen AI on gender equality in the world of work.（§Methodology）
    > The index is constructed at the **task and occupational level**: tasks within occupations are scored for their potential exposure to Gen AI, drawing on a combination of **worker surveys, expert validation, and AI modelling**. These scores are then **aggregated to the level of occupations (ISCO-08, 4-digit)** and classified along a **continuous gradient of exposure, from low to high**.
    > the index is applied to employment data from the **ILO's harmonized microdata collection**, which provides the **gender composition of employment across occupations in 84 countries**.
    > Female-dominated occupations are defined as those with a **female share in employment of at least 75 per cent in at least 25 per cent of countries**. Male-dominated occupations are defined as having a **female share in employment of less than 25 per cent** across the same proportion of countries with reliable data, **(Hegewisch and Liepmann, 2013)**.
    脚注 2（覆盖门槛，决定为什么很多职业没有数）：
    > for an occupation to be included in the analysis, data needed to be available for **at least nine countries**—representing over 10 per cent of the 84 countries with reliable 4-digit ISCO data. Consequently, while the overall dataset covers 84 countries, **not all occupations met this minimum coverage criterion, particularly once data were disaggregated by sex.**

    > Overall, **41 per cent** of employment is potentially exposed to Gen AI technology in high-income countries compared with just **11 per cent** in low-income countries. Furthermore, **9.6 per cent** of employment in high-income countries is in the category of **highest exposure to automation by Gen AI (Gradient 4), compared to just 0.3 per cent in low-income countries** (Gmyrek et al., 2025).
    > **Women are more exposed to Gen AI than men in 88 per cent of countries in the sample.** The highest levels of exposure (**over 40 per cent of female workers**) is found in **small island countries in the Pacific and the Caribbean**, as well as in European countries such as **Switzerland and the United Kingdom**, and in **the Philippines**.
    > Overall, **Europe and Central Asia and Latin America and the Caribbean** are the two regions with the highest levels of exposure for female workers on average, while **Africa and Asia have the lowest exposure.**
  - **教育梯度（本路唯一一处按学历的分组结论，方向在高收入国与低收入国相反）**：
    > Studies find that **in advanced and emerging economies, the share of routine and automatable tasks is higher among older women and those with lower levels of education** (Brussevich et al., 2019). **In contrast, in low- and middle-income countries, Gen AI exposure tends to be higher among women, more educated workers and those in urban areas**, who tend to demonstrate higher digital readiness and greater access to infrastructure (Demombynes et al., 2025, Gmyrek et al., 2024).
  - 男性侧的对照（避免只呈现悲观侧）：
    > While male concentration in male employment is also important (**accounting for 40 to 60 percent of total male employment**), income and regional differences across countries are **less striking** (figure 4). This is because male-dominated occupations are spread more widely across clusters within all three broad economic sectors …
  - **职业结构之外的"低暴露不等于没风险"**：
    > low exposure to Gen AI in low- and middle-income countries primarily reflects the **sectoral composition** of their economies, where employment remains concentrated in agriculture and lower productivity services, **rather than an absence of digital connectivity**. In addition, **task content tends to differ within same occupations located across high- and low-income countries**（Lewandowski, 2025, Gmyrek et al., forthcoming).

  **③ 与 C1／C2 的口径差异（本路要求的对照）**
  - 分母不同：C1 报的是"**就业份额**"（LIC 0.4% / HIC 5.5% automation；augmentation 10.4%–13.4%）；C2 报的是"**四档 gradient 的就业份额**"（全球 gradient 4 = 3.3%，任意 gradient = 24%；HIC 34%）；**C3 同时报两个口径**——"**按职业个数**"（女性主导职业 29% 暴露 vs 男性主导 16%）与"**按就业份额**"（HIC 41% 暴露 / gradient 4 为 9.6%，LIC 0.3%）。**"29% vs 16%"绝不能与"41% vs 11%"当同一类数字用**：前者是"多少个职业"，后者是"多少人"。
  - 阈值不同：C1 用 `0.25/0.5/0.75` 四档 + μ±σ 的两类（automation / augmentation / big unknown）；C2/C3 用 **gradient 1–4 + Minimal Exposure**（表 5 的 μ/σ 规则）。C3 直接沿用 C2，**因此 C3 与 C1 之间不可连线比较**。
  - **会不会给出相反结论？——会，但反在"暴露度=坏"这一步上**：C3 明确写
    > Exposure to Gen AI (gradients 1-4) **does not mean necessarily a technologically induced job redundancy in the near future. Risk of full automation is mostly associated with gradient 4 and even for these occupations, the process should not be assumed as automatic.**
    同时它把"暴露"与"机会"并列：> women are overrepresented in **care-related occupations, which are less likely to be automated due to their reliance on social and interpersonal skills** … **if implemented without adequate consultation, training, interoperability or labour protections, AI can risk intensifying work**, reinforcing existing hierarchies and inequalities, and creating new decent work deficits for a predominantly female workforce (ILO, forthcoming).
    > The more widespread impact of Gen AI lies in the **quality of employment rather than quantity** through its reshaping of tasks, work organisation and skills. **It can intensify workloads, reduce autonomy and introduce bias.** Yet Gen AI also has the potential to improve job quality by **easing physical demands, supporting well-being and enhancing workplace safety and equality**.
    → **低暴露职业（照护、教育）在 C3 里恰恰是"质量风险"更高的一组**：这与"暴露度低＝前景好"直接相反，是"两个指数会不会给出相反结论"的第三层答案（不止 C1 vs C2，同一指数内暴露度与质量风险也反向）。

  **④ 它自陈／可归纳的局限**
    > **This figure, however, captures only part of total employment exposed to Gen AI** …（职业计数口径的自陈局限）
    > **not all occupations met this minimum coverage criterion, particularly once data were disaggregated by sex.**（脚注 2）
    > Its impacts are **not gender-neutral**, often shaped by persistent inequalities …（即：性别差是**结果**不是原因）
    > **Technologies, including Gen AI, are not inherently neutral**, but are embedded within and shaped by societal structures and relations (Spencer, 2018).     > when used as a source of labour market information, **Gen AI tools can reinforce such stereotypes, as they can replicate gendered perceptions of occupational segregation, appropriate remuneration, or the prestige associated with typically male- or female-dominated jobs** (Gmyrek, Lutz and Newlands, 2025). ← **对本项目直接相关的一条：把 GenAI 当岗位信息来源本身会复现性别刻板印象。**
  - 简报里转引的 WEF 数（注意是**预期口径**，见 C4）：
    > Research estimates that AI and information processing technology could lead to a **net gain of around 2 million jobs—creating approximately 11 million roles while displacing 9 million (WEF, 2025)**. At the same time AI adoption has the potential to increase **annual global productivity by between 0.2 and 3.3 percentage points (McKinsey & Company, 2023)**.
    > women represented only **30 per cent of the AI workforce** in 2022, a share that is roughly only **4 percentage points higher than in 2016** (WEF, 2023). … women account for around **40 per cent of STEM professionals globally** … **less than 10 per cent of engineers and software developers**（ILOSTAT, 2023）… **women representing only 29.2 per cent of STEM workers in 2022**（LinkedIn）… a sharp **5.9 per cent decline in the retention of women in STEM** … just one year after graduating（WEF 2023）。
    > In 2023, **21 economies** limited a women's ability to work at night, **49** prohibit women from working in hazardous jobs, and **65** bar women from working in the same industries as men (World Bank, 2023).
    > excessive and unequal care responsibilities keep **708 million women** outside the labour force globally (ILO, 2025). … women perform **more than three-quarters of total unpaid care work, on average 3.2 times more than men** (ILO, 2018).

- **局限与反面证据（本路补充）**：
  1. **它是二手加工件**：暴露度分数完全来自 C2（Gmyrek et al. 2025），本简报自己**不产生新指数**；因此 C1/C2/C3 是**同一条链的三层**，按 §A/条目[16] 的纪律**不构成互证**（`dataset_id` 应相同：`ILO-GenAI-index`）。
  2. **"29% vs 16%"是数职业不是数人**；Annex 显示分母是 82 与 89 个职业。这一类比例最容易被读成"29% 的女性就业暴露"，实际上 C3 的就业加权口径是 HIC 41% / LIC 11%（且那是**按收入组**不是按性别）。
  3. **84 国里没有单独的中国结论**（正文只给收入组与区域；country-level 只点了瑞士、英国、菲律宾、波黑与小岛屿国）。
  4. **性别差的因果方向被作者限定为"隔离的结果"**：原文 "The higher exposure of women is **closely linked to entrenched occupational segregation** and the systemic barriers that sustain it"。它**不是**"女性职业更脆弱"的证据，把它读成后者就是换了因果。
  5. 简报无 p 值、无置信区间、无回归；所有分档来自阈值规则，阈值一动结论就动（C2 的 μ/σ 区间还有重叠）。
  6. **一处内部不一致**：Key points 写 "In high-income countries, **41 per cent** of jobs are exposed"，而 C2 同一口径写 "**34%** of total employment falls within one of the four exposure gradients（HIC）"。两者都引 Gmyrek et al. 2025，**41% 来自性别分解后的女性侧或口径差异**——上屏必须择一并标小节，不能混用。
- **能指到哪条产品决定**：
  1. **给"暴露度 ≠ 方向好坏"补上第三层证据**：C1/C2 说明同一指数两版会反向修订；C3 说明**低暴露职业（照护、教育、手工）恰恰工作质量风险更高**（> "can intensify workloads, reduce autonomy and introduce bias"）。→ 产品判据：**方向卡上禁止出现"暴露度高=差方向 / 暴露度低=好方向"的映射**；若要谈 AI，应改成"任务重组／工作强度／自主性"这类**质量维度**，并标注来源是 ILO 简报的判断而非测量。
  2. **两条可直接进 UI 的硬约束**：
     - 凡引用本类数字，`caliber` 必须区分 **`occupation_count`（29% vs 16%）** 与 **`employment_share`（41% vs 11%）**，UI 文案要写"X 个职业"或"X% 的就业"，不许写"X% 的工作"。

  3. 支撑 README 缺口 3 与 P3 门禁的一条**新风险**：C3 明确点名 **"把 GenAI 当劳动力市场信息来源会复现职业性别刻板印象"**（Gmyrek, Lutz and Newlands, 2025）。这条应写进本项目"LLM 生成岗位/方向描述"的已知偏差清单——与链 3 的 [02-14]（解释不能阻止人接受错误 AI 建议）合成一条：**模型侧偏差 + 用户侧过度采信，两头都要防。**

- **局限与反面证据（本路补充）**：
  1. **它是"雇主的期待"，不是"发生的事实"**：所有数字的主语是 `employers expect / survey respondents / plan to`。170M/92M/78M 是"把 1,043 家企业高管对未来五年的预测乘上 ILO 就业人数"得到的**外推值**，不是任何统计观测。**产品的"事实区"不许出现任何一个 WEF 数字。**

  4. **覆盖缺口极大**：报告原句是 Future of Jobs 数据只对应 **1.18 billion** 从业者，而 ILO 库（含中国乘数）是 **2.76 billion**，**"the remaining 1.58 billion employees correspond to roles for which the Future of Jobs Survey did not collect sufficient data to reliably estimate net growth"**。（1.58/2.76 ≈ 57% 是本记录的**推算**，非原句）也就是说本报告的岗位清单**不覆盖大半全球就业**，产品不能把它当"全职业前景表"用。
  5. **同一指标在同报告内有两套值**（AI 相关：Key findings 的 "half reorient / two-thirds hire AI skills / 40% reduce" vs §4.3 的 "49% / 69%+62% / 41%"；技能颠覆 39% 在不同分册被写成 44%（Türkiye）等），引用必须带小节。
  6. **"skill instability 从 44% 降到 39%"与"AI 86% 将改变业务"并存**——不能只取后一句做"AI 冲击加剧"的叙事；报告自己的方向是**冲击预期在放缓**。
  7. 与 C1/C2 的关系：WEF 的岗位清单是 **O*NET→ISCO-08 映射**，ILO 指数是 **ISCO-08 任务打分**，两者职业可对齐，但**一个问的是"雇主觉得会不会增减"，一个算的是"任务技术上能不能被做"**。两者对同一职业给出不排序的结论（例如 Data Entry Clerks 在 WEF 降、在 ILO 属 gradient 4；Software developers 在 WEF 增、在 ILO 2025 被上调进高暴露）——**"高暴露"与"岗位增长"可以同时为真**，这正是不能把暴露度当判决的最直观对照。

  **① 总量保守估计数值（本条的产品用途就是"封顶"）**
    > Using existing estimates on exposure to AI and productivity improvements at the task level, these macroeconomic effects appear **nontrivial but modest—no more than a 0.66% increase in total factor productivity (TFP) over 10 years**. … even these estimates could be **exaggerated** … Consequently, predicted TFP gains over the next 10 years are **even more modest and are predicted to be less than 0.53%.**（Abstract）
    推导链（§3，每一步的输入都可核）：
    > I take **Eloundou et al.'s estimates** of tasks that are exposed to AI (without distinguishing automation vs. task complementarities). I then aggregate this to the occupational level and **weight the importance of each occupation by its wage bill share** in the US economy. This calculation implies that **20% of US labor tasks are exposed to AI**. I then use **Svanberg et al.'s estimate** for computer vision tasks that, **among all exposed tasks, 23% can be profitably performed by AI** (for the rest, the authors estimate that **the costs would exceed the benefits**). I take the **average labor cost savings to be 27%**—the average of the estimates in **Noy and Zhang (2023) and Brynjolfsson et al. (2023)**—and turn this into overall cost savings using industry labor shares, which imply **average overall cost savings of 14.4%.**
    > TFP gains over 10 years = **GDP share impacted by AI over the next 10 years × average cost savings of impact tasks.**
    > TFP gains over the next 10 years = **0.046 × 0.144 ≃ 0.0066.**
    > In other words … TFP will be higher by **0.66 percentage points in 10 years, or annual TFP growth will be higher by around 0.064%.** This is a **nontrivial, but modest effect**, and certainly **much less than both the revolutionary changes some are predicting** and the less hyperbolic but still substantial improvements forecast by **Goldman Sachs and the McKinsey Global Institute**.
    > If we were to consider the higher productivity numbers from **Peng et al.**, 0.144 would be replaced by 0.193, and the 10-year TFP gains would be **0.89%**, instead of 0.66%.
    > … in Svanberg et al.'s scenarios where costs for computer vision decline very rapidly, such as **10% a year**, the fraction of tasks that are feasibly automated may be as high as **30%**. This would raise the GDP share impacted by AI to approximately **6%**, and correspondingly increase the TFP gains over the next 10 years to about **0.9%. Note that even this number is quite modest**, and moreover, **10% per annum cost declines is quite aggressive**.
    easy/hard task 修正（§3.2）：
    > Using a range of **(speculative) assumptions**, I estimate an **upper bound of 73% easy tasks** among Eloundou et al.'s exposed tasks. I suppose that **productivity gains in hard tasks will be approximately one quarter of the easy ones**. This leads to an updated, more modest increase in TFP and GDP in the next 10 years that can be **upper bounded by 0.53% and 0.90%, respectively.**
    > I take the **27% labor cost savings** … to apply to easy-to-learn tasks … Here I take those productivity gains [hard tasks] to be **7%** … **Noy and Zhang (2023) … led to cost savings of about 40%** … the customer service tasks in Brynjolfsson et al. (2023) … had cost savings of only **14%**.
    > **TFP gains over the next 10 years = 0.033 × 0.144 + (0.046 − 0.033) × 0.037 ≃ 0.0053.**
    GDP 与资本份额：
    > Using the capital share for the entire private business sector, **0.43**, this implies that GDP gains will be equal to the TFP gains multiplied by **1.75** (≃ 1/(1 − 0.43)). Hence taking the baseline estimate of an increase in TFP of 0.66%, I obtain a first estimate for **GDP growth due to AI of 1.16% over 10 years, or taking the presence of hard tasks into account, a lower estimate of 0.93%.**
    > This leads to an **upper bound GDP impact of 1.4%** … (or **1.56%** when this distinction is not drawn). GDP therefore increases **substantially more than average wages**, and as a result, **the capital share of national income increases by about 0.31 percentage points.** This confirms that **inequality between capital and labor is likely to rise** as a result of the rollout of AI.
    > … my calculations suggest that the **GDP boost within the next 10 years should also be modest, in the range of 0.93% − 1.16% over 10 years in total**, provided that the investment increase resulting from AI is modest, and in the range of **1.4% − 1.56% in total, if there is a large investment boom.**
    **被它当场比下去的那些"大数字"（本路要的是这组对照）**：
    > **Goldman Sachs (2023) predicts a 7% increase in global GDP, equivalent to $7 trillion, and a 1.5% per annum increase in US productivity growth over a 10-year period.** Recent **McKinsey Global Institute (2023)** forecasts suggest that generative AI could offer a boost as large as **$17.1 to $25.6 trillion** to the global economy … They reckon that the overall impact of AI and other automation technologies could produce up to a **1.5 − 3.4 percentage point rise in average annual GDP growth in advanced economies** over the coming decade.（§1）
    > **Korinek and Suh (2024) predict a "baseline" GDP growth of 100% over the next 10 years**, and also entertain the possibility of much higher "aggressive" AGI growth rates, such as a **300% increase in GDP**.（§1 脚注 1）
    方法论限制（为什么必须保守）：
    > **2. Any major technology creates adjustment costs when adopted at large scale** … Greenwood and Yorukoglu (1997) and Brynjolfsson et al. (2021) … have argued that productivity gains will take a **J-shaped pattern**, and the former paper predicts that **the flat part of the J-curve lasts no less than 20 years for digital technologies. If so, the 14.4% overall cost reductions may be a significant overestimate for the next 10 years.**
    > These considerations make me conclude that **even the 0.66% increase in TFP within the next 10 years due to AI is likely to be an upper bound** on this technology's medium-run effects.
