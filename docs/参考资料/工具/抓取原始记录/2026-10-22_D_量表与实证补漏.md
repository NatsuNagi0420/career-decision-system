## D1 · 生涯适应力短量表 CAAS-SF 在中国初入职场毕业生上的验证（Song / Hashim / Xu / Ling, 2023）

- **名称**：Career adapt-ability scale – short form: Validation among early career stage of Chinese university graduates
- **作者与年份**：Chunze Song, Shahabuddin Bin Hashim, Xinpeng Xu, Hairong Ling · 2023-01-27 · *Frontiers in Psychology* 14:1110287（Brief Research Report）
- **DOI 与 URL**：DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110287` ｜ https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110287/full ｜ PDF https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1110287/pdf
- **HTTP 与字节**：PDF **200 / 609,939 B**，`file` 报 PDF 1.6 / 25 页；pdftotext 抽出 45,438 字符，表 1–3、图 1、参考文献齐全（页码 01–07 连续）。
- **读到什么程度**：**一手 · 论文全文**（CC BY 开放获取；已通读方法与结果，数字逐字来自正文）。
- **逐字原文摘录**：
  - 样本（Materials and methods → Participants）：
    > "This study focuses primarily on graduates of colleges and universities in Jiangsu Province or those who work in Jiangsu Province…… Participants in the questionnaire survey are recent graduates who have worked for three months to five years."
    > "In November 2021, the test link was distributed…… 700 surveys were sent out and gathered…… After screening and removing invalid questionnaires, **571 valid questionnaires were recovered, representing an effective recovery rate of 81.57%**."
    > "62.52 percent of the 571 valid data obtained were female, and 46.58 percent were from rural areas…… The respondents work in 26 provinces in China, with Jiangsu (54.47%), Shanghai (12.26%), and Beijing (4.72%) ranking as the top three."
    - 抽样方式自陈：`non-random sample methods, including convenience sampling and snowball sampling`。
  - 工具（Measures）：
    > "The **12-item CAAS-SF scale updated by Maggiori et al. (2015)**, which was derived from the 24-item CAAS (Savickas and Porfeli, 2012)…… this study cites the Chinese version of the Career Adapt-Abilities Scale-China Form amended by **Hou (Hou et al., 2012)**."
    > "The scale retains four aspects: career concern, career control, career curiosity, and career confidence. **Each test aspect consists of three exam items, for a total of twelve. Adoption of the Likert5-level scoring approach.**"
  - 信度（Table 1 正文）：
    > "the overall structure of CAAS-SF has a dependability of **0.944**, which is more than that of **concern (0.869), control (0.849), curiosity (0.867), and confidence (0.887)**…… the CITC value of each analysis item is greater than 0.50…… after any item is deleted, the reliability coefficient will not increase obviously, indicating that all items should be kept."
  - 结构效度（Factor analysis）：
    > "The value of **KMO is 0.94**, the **Bartlett chi-square number is 5081.765**, and the p-value is around 0…… Through principal component analysis, four factors were recovered…… the explanation rate of variance for each factor after rotation was **21.75, 18.90, 18.84, and 20.61%**, and the explanation rate of **cumulative variance was 80.10% > 50%**."
    > "AVE square root values of the 4 factors of CAAS-SF were **0.831, 0.808, 0.829, and 0.854**, respectively…… AVE values…… are all larger than 0.5…… combined reliability CR values all seem to be greater than 0.7."
  - 模型拟合（Structural model fitting analysis）：
    > "the fitting value of the structural model is **χ²/df = 3.739**, slightly over the cut-off value 3 and lower than 5…… and **RMSEA = 0.069**, which is between 0.05 and 0.08, while **CFI (0.973), TLI (0.964), IFI (0.973), and NFI (0.974)** are all higher than 0.9."
    > "the curiosity's factor loading rated first (**0.95**), and the confidence's factor loading ranked second (**0.93**), both of which were greater than those of concern (**0.85**) and control (**0.89**)."
  - 组间差异（卡方检验，非测量不变性）：
    > "CAAS-SF scores varied with gender (**χ² = 47.589, p = 0.114**), place of origin (χ² = 39.179, p = 0.372), educational background (χ² = 70.810, p = 0.584), school category (χ² = 110.848, p = 0.496), major category (χ² = 82.363, p = 0.237) and graduation time (χ² = 154.428, p = 0.342) **had no statistical significance**."
  - 局限（Limitations and future implications，作者自陈）：
    > "this is still a **cross-sectional study**; therefore, future research should focus on the longitudinal link…… the current sample comprises graduates of different majors from four different types of institutions in China, but **Jiangsu is the main province**…… the reliability and validity of the scale can be confirmed further by taking into account various cultural backgrounds……"
- **局限与反面证据**：
  1. **横断、非随机（便利+滚雪球）**，且 54.47% 集中在江苏——不能把它当"全国毕业生基线"，只能当"中国语境下 12 题 CAAS-SF 可用"的证据。
  2. **没有做纵向/多组测量不变性检验**。正文里的"性别/专业/毕业时间无显著差异"是 **Kruskal-Wallis/卡方均值差异检验**，不是 configural→metric→scalar 的不变性检验。凡是要跨组比较得分水平，本条**给不出授权**（与 01 笔记 [01-06] Savickas「标量不变性不成立」叠加，进一步只能做个体内前后差）。
  3. **无重测信度、无前测—后测敏感度证据**。它对"一周内能不能测出干预变化"沉默。
  4. 反面结果：本研究性别差异**不显著**，与 Hou 等（2012）、Yu 等（2019）"男高于女"的中文结果**相反**——提示基线的性别差在不同样本不稳定，试点 10–15 人前后测不能拿性别差当"改善"。
  5. 这是一份 **Brief Research Report**（25 页 PDF 里正文约 7 页），量表逐题条目放在附录 Table A1，正文未列全部题干。
- **能指到哪条产品决定**：
  - **README 缺口 1 / 索引 链 1**：链 1 现用 CAAS-CSF（Hou 版 12 题，[01-08] 在大学生样本）。本条把**另一个 12 题短表（Maggiori CAAS-SF）落在"已工作 3 个月–5 年的中国毕业生"上**，四因子结构与 α 可核（总 0.944，各维 0.849–0.887，每维 3 题）。所以试点如果样本含"已入职 1 年内"用户，**12 题、每维 3 题**这个"前后测最短可用题数"在中国初入职场人群上有直接证据支撑，不必只依赖大学生样本外推。
  - **成本核算**：12 题 × 前后两次 ≈ 与链 1 现方案同量级（约 3 分钟/次）。
  - **不得越界**：本表给出的是适应力水平，不得映射为"职业适合度"（`PROJECT_GOAL` 目标二）；且因无标量不变性证据，**只做同一人前后差，不做跨组/跨版本均值比较**（与 [01-06] 同一口径）。
- **本地文件名**：`01_职业决策理论与量表/11_CAAS-SF中国毕业生验证.pdf`；抽取文本 `工具/原文文本/11_CAAS-SF中国毕业生验证.txt`

## D2 · CDMSE-SF 中文版在中国大学生上的信效度复测（Dan Linge & Azlina Mohd Kosnin, 2024）

- **名称**：Revisiting Reliability and Validity of Career Decision-making Self-efficacy Short-form Scale among College Students in China
- **作者与年份**：Dan Linge, Azlina Mohd Kosnin · 2024 · *International Journal of Academic Research in Business and Social Sciences*（IJARBSS）14(5)（出版方 The International HCR Press / hrmars.com）
- **DOI 与 URL**：DOI `10.6007/IJARBSS/v14-i5/21556` ｜ 摘要页 https://doi.org/10.6007/IJARBSS/v14-i5/21556 ｜ PDF https://hrmars.com/papers_submitted/21556/revisiting-reliability-and-validity-of-career-decision-making-self-efficacy-short-form-scale-among-college-students-in-china.pdf
- **HTTP 与字节**：摘要页 **200 / 60,517 B**；PDF **200 / 369,020 B**，`file` 报 PDF 1.7 / 11 页；pdftotext 抽出 34,626 字符，正文含方法、EFA、CFA、结果与讨论。
- **读到什么程度**：**一手 · 论文全文**（开放获取 diamond；数字逐字来自正文）。**来源档次存疑**：期刊 IJARBSS 属低门槛开放刊，非 SSCI 主库；结论只作"中文样本上有人这样报"的旁证，不单独当决策依据（见"局限"）。
- **逐字原文摘录**：
  - 样本（Method → Participants）：
    > "permission was obtained from a comprehensive university located in a medium-sized city in western China…… 10 classes attended by third- and fourth-year students…… a total of **510 online questionnaires** were distributed…… **A total of 483 validly answered questionnaires were retained**, resulting in an effective recovery rate of 94.7%."
    > "**Sample 1…… A total of 280 participants**…… 82 males and 198 females. **Sample 2 consisted of 203 participants**…… 59 males and 144 females."（EFA 用样本1，CFA 用样本2）
  - 工具（Instrument）：
    > "the Chinese version of the CDMSE-SF by **Long (2003)**…… includes all **25 items** of the CDMES-SF Betz et al (1996), encompassing the five dimensions of **problem solving (PS), goal setting (GS), planning (PL), self-assessment (SA), and occupational information (OI)**. Each item was rated on a **five-point scale**……"
  - 信度（Reliability Analysis）：
    > "The calculation of the coefficient for the total scale indicated a high level of reliability with an **alpha value of 0.967**. The subscales also demonstrated good internal consistency with values of **0.837 (SA), 0.846 (OI), 0.885 (GS), 0.867 (PL) and 0.858 (PS)**…… the split-half reliability coefficients…… ranged from **0.817 to 0.947**."
  - 结构效度（EFA，样本1）：
    > "the **KMO value of 0.959** exceeds 0.9…… Bartlett's test of sphericity (**χ² = 5285.752**)…… p < 0.01…… five common factors were extracted…… **could explain 71.11% of the variance**…… several items (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 18, 19) did not correspond to the dimension…… were considered for deletion."
  - 结构效度（CFA，样本2，N=203）：
    > "A confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) was conducted on a separate dataset (**N=203**) using AMOS 24…… χ²/df=**1,837**, GFI=**0.937**, CFI=**0.975**, RMSEA=**0.064**. These results indicated that the structural validity of the revised scale was good."
  - 关键结论（与 Hampton 分歧）：
    > "the deletion of **12 items** that did not fit into the dimensions. Afterward, the **remaining 13 items could still be classified into the 5 dimensions**, and the **5-factor structure was also supported** by the results of the CFA. **This differs from the results of both Long (2003) and Hampton (2005)** in their studies of Chinese college students."
  - 对 Hampton 的转述（作者原文，便于对照 [01-02]）：
    > "Hampton (2005)…… The **256 Chinese university students**…… did not support the initial 25-item CDMES-SF…… Ultimately, only the **13-item three-factor model was retained**…… CFA of the three-factor model on another sample group (**157 university students**) were favorable."
- **局限与反面证据**：
  1. **13 题"最短可用题数"在两份中文研究里数字一致、因子拆解却不一致**：Hampton 2005 = 13 题**三因子**；本文 = 13 题**五因子**。这不是可忽略的细节——它直接决定试点能不能"分维报分"。低质量刊 + 单一院校 + 只此一篇支持五因子，**五因子这条不足以推翻链 1 现用的"三路分别报分"**。
  2. **13 题里删掉 12 题（25→13）**，且作者承认"not all the topics align with the understanding of Chinese university students"；把 13 题硬分进 5 个维度，平均每维约 2.6 题，**单维题数过少、无法可靠算维度分**，这与 [01-02] 的谨慎结论（"中文标签只能当工作性标签"）方向一致。
  3. **α 报告对象不清**：0.967 与五个子量表 α（.837–.885）是**在 25 题版本上算的**，正文未对删题后的 13 题版本单独列表重算 α——引用"13 题版 α"时应回原文再核，本条**不声称 13 题版有独立 α**。
  4. **横断、便利抽样、单一西部院校、三/四年级**；无重测信度、无干预敏感度证据；未报测量不变性。
  5. **出版与审稿质量风险**：IJARBSS/HCR Press 常被列为低门槛开放刊，方法学错误风险高。本文只作"中文大学生 CDSES-SF 结构有争议"的**存在性证据**，不作主依据。
- **能指到哪条产品决定**：
  - **README 缺口 1 / 索引 链 1 第 1、3 条**：链 1 现写"CDSE-SF 25 题五维在中国大学生样本不成立（N=256，[01-02]），13 题三因子才成立"。本条给出**同一题量（13 题）、不同因子数（五因子）的对立结果**——所以链 1 的正确表述应是"**13 题在两份中文研究里都可用，但到底是 3 因子还是 5 因子没有定论**"，据此**只报一条'决策清晰度'轴**更稳妥（链 1 第 3 条），分维报分要标"结构未定"。
  - **给"前后测最短可用题数"补一个中国大学生数**：CDMSE-SF 中文落地最短可用 ≈ **13 题**（本文 N=483，与 [01-02] 一致）。
- **本地文件名**：`01_职业决策理论与量表/12_CDMSE-SF中国大学生复测.pdf`；抽取文本 `工具/原文文本/12_CDMSE-SF中国大学生复测.txt`

## D3 · CDDQ 中文版（吴 2016 版）+ CDMSE-SF 中文版（Long 2003 版）在中国研究生样本上的同时落地（Bi / Mou / Wang / Liao, 2023）

- **名称**：The relationship between professional self-concept and career decision-making difficulties among postgraduate nursing students in China: the mediating role of career decision-making self-efficacy
- **作者与年份**：Yaping Bi, Shaoyu Mou, Ge Wang, Mingyan Liao · 2023 · *Frontiers in Psychology* 14:1198974
- **DOI 与 URL**：DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1198974` ｜ https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1198974/full ｜ PDF https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1198974/pdf
- **HTTP 与字节**：PDF **200 / 383,426 B**，`file` 报 PDF 1.6 / 28 页；pdftotext 抽出 44,640 字符，表 1–4、图 1 齐全。
- **读到什么程度**：**一手 · 论文全文**（CC BY 开放获取）。**性质提示**：这是一篇**应用/中介研究**，不是量表再验证；价值在于它把 CDDQ 中文版与 CDMSE-SF 中文版**同时跑在一个中国研究生样本上并报了 α**，给"量表在中文样本落地"补一个可核的 α 数与分数。
- **逐字原文摘录**：
  - 样本（2.1 Participants）：
    > "a stratified random sampling method was conducted to select **276 postgraduate nursing students from 25 universities in seven administrative regions of China**…… The mean age…… was 24.75 (SD = 2.85)…… The majority were female (92.03%)…… **276 valid questionnaires were finally collected, with a valid recovery rate of 95.17%**."（分层随机抽样；最小样本量按 Kendall 原则推算为 120）
  - CDDQ 中文版（2.2.2）：
    > "measured using a Chinese version of the Career Decision-making Difficulty Questionnaire developed by **(Wu et al., 2016)**. The questionnaire consists of **36 items across three domains: Lack of Readiness (nine items), Lack of Information (fourteen items), and Inconsistent Information (three items)**…… 5-point Likert…… The questionnaire's Cronbach's alpha was **0.816**. **In this study, Cronbach's alpha was 0.933.**"
  - CDMSE-SF 中文版（2.2.3）：
    > "measured using a Chinese version of the Career Decision-making Self-efficacy scale revised by **Long (2003)**. The scale consists of **25 items across five domains: self-appraisal, goal selection, gathering information, planning, and problem-solving (five items each)**…… 5-point Likert…… The scale's Cronbach's alpha was **0.89**. **In this study, Cronbach's alpha was 0.969.**"
  - 关键关系（结果/摘要）：
    > "The score for career decision-making difficulties was **2.84 (SD = 0.54)**…… career decision-making self-efficacy had a significant **negative correlation with career decision-making difficulties (r = −0.551, p < 0.01)**…… professional self-concept…… negative correlation with career decision-making difficulties (r = −0.496, p < 0.01)."
    > 中介：Career decision-making self-efficacy 在自我概念与决策困难间起部分中介，效应值 −0.253（Bootstrap 95% CI −0.349, −0.156），占总效应 **53.82%**。
- **局限与反面证据**：
  1. **样本是护理学研究生（92% 女性、平均 24.75 岁）**，不是本科应届毕业生/初入职场主流人群；分数与 α 只能说明"这套中文版在中国研究生里可用"，不能直接搬到 22 岁本科生基线。
  2. 这里的 **CDDQ 中文版是 Wu(2016) 的 36 题三域版**，与 [01-03]（Creed & Wong 2006 的 19 题两因子版）**不是同一版本**——尤其"不一致信息"在本版仅 **3 题**、"准备不足"仍保留 9 题；而 [01-03] 的结论是"准备不足"α=.29 应弃用。两版并存说明 **CDDQ 中文版没有统一权威版本**，引用时必须写清是哪一版（这是本条最重要的反面信息）。
  3. 只报了**总量表 α**，没有分维度 α、没有做 CFA/不变性检验；正文对 36 题三域结构是"沿用"而非"重验"。
  4. 横断自报数据，线上问卷（问卷星），无重测信度。
- **能指到哪条产品决定**：
  - **README 缺口 1 / 索引 链 1 第 3 条、链 2**：`r = −0.551` 是中国样本上"效能↑则困难↓"的直接相关数，与 [01-04] 美国样本的 −0.50/−0.52 同向——支撑"**只报一条'决策清晰度'轴**（困难与效能是同一轴两端），不并列当两个成效"。
  - **README 缺口 2 / 链 2**：本文用的 CDDQ 三域里"信息不足 / 不一致信息"两个域，正是链 2 想借的"证据缺口 / 冲突证据"两把可测标准；但**版本不统一（36 题 Wu 版 vs 19 题 Creed 版）**这条要写进"标准从哪来"的口径说明，防止把两个版本的分数当同一把尺。
  - **不得越界**：α=.933/.969 是量表内部一致性，不构成"适合度"，也不得进入岗位比较矩阵。

## D4 · 太极运动干预提升中国大学生生涯适应力（随机对照；Wang / Zhai / Sun, 2024）

- **名称**：Enhancing career adaptability in college students: a Tai Chi-based sports intervention study
